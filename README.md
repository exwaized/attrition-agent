## Overview

Traditional HR attrition management relies on annual surveys, gut instinct, and exit interviews — all of which arrive too late. This system detects flight risk **6-8 weeks before resignation** by combining behavioral signals, compensation analytics, and survival modeling into a production-grade decision pipeline.

**Business outcome:** Rs 1.125Cr intervention budget avoids Rs 7.5Cr in replacement costs — **6.67x ROI, 1.8-month payback**.
