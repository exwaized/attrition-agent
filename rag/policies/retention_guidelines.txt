This is the employee retention guidelines and Market Alignment Program policy for Jio Platforms HR.

RETENTION GUIDELINES AND MARKET ALIGNMENT PROGRAM

Section 1 - Market Alignment Program (MAP)
Employees with a compa-ratio below 0.85 are eligible for an off-cycle salary correction under the Market Alignment Program.
The compa-ratio is calculated as the employee's actual salary divided by the market midpoint for their band, department, and circle tier.
Manager must raise a MAP request with approved budget codes within the current quarter for eligible employees.
MAP corrections are processed within 30 days of request approval.
Maximum MAP correction in a single cycle is 25 percent of current base salary.
Employees who received a MAP correction are not eligible for another MAP correction within 12 months.

Section 2 - Retention Bonus Program
Employees classified as High or Critical attrition risk and rated 3.5 or above are eligible for a spot retention bonus.
Spot bonus range: Rs 15000 to Rs 75000 depending on band and risk severity.
Band 3 and 4: Rs 15000 to Rs 30000
Band 5: Rs 30000 to Rs 50000
Band 6 and above: Rs 50000 to Rs 75000
Manager must submit retention bonus request with HRBP co-approval.
Retention bonus is paid immediately upon approval and does not affect annual bonus calculation.

Section 3 - Critical Attrition Risk Protocol
Employees with attrition probability above 0.70 as assessed by the HR analytics system are classified as Critical Attrition Risk.
Manager must be notified within 24 hours of Critical classification.
Manager must conduct a 1-on-1 retention conversation within 5 working days.
HRBP must be looped in within 7 days.
A retention action plan must be documented and filed within 14 days.
Progress review must occur at 30 days and 60 days.

Section 4 - Peer Contagion Protocol
When 3 or more employees from the same team leave within any 90 day period, a Contagion Risk Assessment is triggered.
HRBP must assess remaining team members within 10 days.
All remaining team members with tenure under 24 months in the affected team are automatically elevated to Medium risk.
Skip-level manager must hold a team town hall within 14 days.
Hiring to backfill must begin within 21 days to prevent further morale deterioration.

Section 5 - Post-Appraisal Retention Window
The 60 day period following appraisal result communication is the highest voluntary resignation window.
Employees who received below-median hike and have a performance rating of 3.5 or above must be proactively engaged by their manager within 14 days of appraisal results.
HRBP must review all resignation submissions received within 60 days of appraisal for systemic patterns.
