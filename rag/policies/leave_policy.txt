This is the leave policy document for Jio Platforms HR.

LEAVE POLICY

Section 1 - Annual Leave Entitlement
All permanent employees are entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave must be applied for at least 3 working days in advance except in emergency situations.
Unused leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 30 days per year.
Leave encashment is permitted for a maximum of 15 days per year during the annual appraisal cycle.

Section 2 - Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.
Medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days.
Sick leave cannot be carried forward or encashed.

Section 3 - Team Health Check Trigger
Teams experiencing more than 15 percent headcount reduction within any 90 day period qualify for a mandatory team health check.
The team health check must be facilitated by the HRBP within 14 days of the trigger condition being met.
A skip-level meeting between the remaining team members and the skip-level manager must be scheduled within 2 weeks.
The HRBP must document findings and submit a team stabilisation plan to HR leadership within 30 days.

Section 4 - Excessive Leave Pattern
Employees taking more than 5 days unplanned leave in any 30 day period are flagged for manager attention.
Manager must conduct a wellbeing check-in within 5 working days of the flag being raised.
Repeated patterns across 2 consecutive months must be escalated to HRBP with documentation.

Section 5 - Attendance and Login Monitoring
Employees with login frequency dropping below 60 percent of their 90-day baseline for 2 consecutive weeks are flagged for engagement review.
Manager is notified automatically and must conduct a 1-on-1 within 7 days.
HRBP is looped in if pattern continues for more than 30 days without resolution.
