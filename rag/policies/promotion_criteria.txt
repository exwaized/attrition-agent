This is the promotion criteria and career progression policy for Jio Platforms HR.

PROMOTION CRITERIA AND CAREER PROGRESSION POLICY

Section 1 - Eligibility for Band Promotion
Employees must have completed a minimum of 18 months at their current band before being considered for promotion.
Employees must have received a performance rating of 4.0 or above in at least 2 of the last 3 appraisal cycles.
Manager nomination is required. Promotions cannot be self-nominated.
Final approval requires HRBP sign-off and HR leadership review for Band 5 and above.

Section 2 - Mandatory Escalation Rule
Cases where an eligible employee has been pending promotion review for more than 18 months must be escalated to HRBP with documented justification for delay.
Failure to escalate within 21 months constitutes a process violation and must be reported to HR leadership.
HRBP must resolve escalated cases within 60 days of receipt.
Employees in escalated cases must be informed of their status and expected timeline within 14 days of escalation.

Section 3 - High Performer Retention Protocol
Employees rated 4.2 or above for 2 consecutive cycles who have not been promoted within 20 months are classified as High Performer Retention Risk.
These cases automatically trigger a retention discussion between the manager, HRBP, and the employee within 30 days.
Retention package options include accelerated promotion track, spot bonus, role enhancement, and market alignment salary correction.
HR head must be informed of all High Performer Retention Risk cases monthly.

Section 4 - Promotion Velocity Standards
Expected promotion timelines by band:
Band 3 to Band 4: 18 to 24 months average
Band 4 to Band 5: 24 to 36 months average
Band 5 to Band 6: 36 to 48 months average
Band 6 to Band 7: 48 to 60 months average
Employees significantly outside these timelines must be reviewed by HRBP.

Section 5 - Post-Promotion Support
Newly promoted employees must receive a role transition meeting with their manager within 14 days.
A 90 day check-in by HRBP is mandatory for all Band 5 and above promotions.
Compensation adjustment accompanying promotion must be processed within 30 days of promotion effective date.
